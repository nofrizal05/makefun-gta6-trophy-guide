Roadmap to Platinum

One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10. No online trophies, no difficulty setting, no mission medals — the platinum is long, not hard. Work the phases in order; everything after the story is free-roam cleanup.

    Missable Trophies

    Good news: by every early guide, only one trophy in the whole game can be missed. Everything else — story trophies, side strands, collectibles, grinds — stays available in free roam after the story. The one exception needs doing in Chapter 1.

    Save discipline (no mission replay)

    GTA 6 has no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one more before the final mission. That is your only way back if you miss an optional interaction or want to revisit a story choice. Optional missions marked with an hourglass icon can disappear when a chapter advances — they do not block any trophy, but they are easy to lose.
